From raw sales to a decision

A four-step pipeline that connects predictions to the actions a manager actually takes.

1

Clean & analyse

Historical sales and inventory are de-duplicated, gaps filled and outliers capped before modelling.

2

Engineer features

Time, lag and rolling-average features capture weekday patterns, seasonality and momentum.

3

Predict demand

A gradient-boosted model forecasts the next 7-day demand for every product.

4

Flag risk

Forecast vs. stock and lead time produce High / Medium / Overstocked / Safe labels and reorder quantities.
